# Larkspur Assurance — Customer Decision Letter Standards

**Document ID:** LTR-02
**Version:** 1.4
**Effective:** 1 March 2026

## 1. Every settlement or decline letter must include

1. Claim ID and policy number.
2. A plain-English summary of the decision.
3. A table of every item claimed, showing: amount claimed, amount paid, and — for anything not paid in full — the reason in plain English **and** the policy clause reference.
4. Where an excess has been deducted, the section and the amount.
5. Where currency conversion was used, the basis (amount debited on statement, or reference rate and date).
6. For any decline based on facts (e.g. unattended property), the evidence relied on, quoted or summarised fairly, and an invitation to provide further information if the customer believes the facts are wrong.
7. The payment amount, date and reference (`LARKSPUR {claim_id}`), and the last four digits of the account paid.
8. Complaint and Financial Ombudsman Service rights (policy Section 15).

## 2. Tone

- Write in plain English. Do not use the word "fraud" or imply dishonesty. Do not mention fraud scores or QA flags.
- Explain, don't just cite. "Your phone was not covered because ..." followed by the clause.
- Be specific with numbers — the customer must be able to reproduce every figure.

## 3. Timing

The letter must be sent within 1 working day of the payment being issued, and no later than 5 working days after the decision.
