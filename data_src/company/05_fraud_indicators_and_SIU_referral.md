# Larkspur Assurance — Fraud Indicators & SIU Referral (Travel)

**Document ID:** FRD-03
**Version:** 3.0
**Effective:** 1 January 2026
**Owner:** Special Investigations Unit (SIU)

---

## 1. Purpose

Indicators help us decide which claims need a closer look. **An indicator is not proof of fraud.** Most claims with one or two indicators are genuine. Never decline a claim, or tell a customer they are suspected of fraud, because of indicators alone. Only SIU may make a finding under policy condition GC.2.

## 2. Scoring

Add the weight of every indicator triggered. Each indicator counts **once per claim**, however many times it occurs.

| Score | Band | Action |
|---|---|---|
| 0–24 | Green | Proceed normally |
| 25–39 | Amber | Proceed; flag for post-payment QA sample; record indicators in Evidence Pack |
| 40+ | Red | Do not pay. REFER to SIU with Evidence Pack |

## 3. Indicators

| ID | Indicator | Weight |
|---|---|---|
| F1 | Policy purchased ≤ 7 days before departure, or ≤ 3 days before the first incident | 20 |
| F2 | Material inconsistency between the claimant's accounts (claim form / statement / claimant translation) and independent evidence (police report, carrier, medical) | 15 |
| F3 | Theft reported to police more than 24 hours after discovery | 15 |
| F4 | Duplicate receipt or duplicate line item on the same claim | 10 |
| F5 | Claim includes a single gadget or valuable claimed at > £500 | 5 |
| F6 | Two or more travel claims by the same insured in the previous 24 months | 15 |
| F7 | Receipts that are handwritten, lack a merchant tax ID, or have round-number totals for multiple items | 10 |
| F8 | Proof of ownership is dated after the claimed incident | 30 |
| F9 | Bank details on the claim differ from the verified payee account on file | 25 |
| F10 | Claimed amounts on the claim form exceed the receipts by > 20% in any section (after removing duplicates) | 10 |
| F11 | Medical documents from a provider that cannot be verified (no registration number, no address) | 20 |

## 4. Notes for assessors

4.1 **F2** applies when the difference is *material* to the decision (e.g. whether property was unattended, when an item was bought, who travelled). Minor wording differences are not material.

4.2 **F4** is triggered by the duplicate itself; it does not matter whether the claimant explains it.

4.3 **F6**: count only claims made in the 24 months before the current claim's notification date, using the policy record's claims history.

4.4 Record every indicator **considered** (triggered or not) in the Evidence Pack, so reviewers can see what was checked.

4.5 An indicator relating to a line item that is being declined on coverage grounds still counts toward the score.
