# Larkspur Assurance Ltd — Travel Insurance Policy Wording

**Wording reference:** TRV-2024 v3
**Applies to:** Single Trip and Annual Multi-Trip policies with a policy start date between 1 January 2024 and 31 December 2025
**Status:** SUPERSEDED for new policies by TRV-2026 v1 from 1 January 2026. Remains the governing wording for policies issued under reference TRV-2024 v3.

> This document, together with your Certificate of Insurance, forms your contract of insurance with Larkspur Assurance Ltd. Please read it alongside your Certificate, which shows the cover level you chose, the people insured and the dates of cover. Marketing material, websites and sales literature do not form part of this contract.

---

## 1. About this policy

1.1 This is a contract between the person(s) named on the Certificate of Insurance ("you", "your") and Larkspur Assurance Ltd ("we", "us", "our").

1.2 We will provide the cover described in this wording for the cover level (Silver, Gold or Platinum) shown on your Certificate, subject to the limits, excesses, exclusions and conditions set out here.

1.3 Cover applies only to the insured persons named on the Certificate of Insurance. Costs incurred by, or on behalf of, any person not named on the Certificate are not covered, even where those costs were paid by an insured person.

1.4 The law of England and Wales applies to this contract. All communication will be in English.

---

## 2. Definitions

Wherever the following words appear in **bold** in this wording they have the meanings below.

2.1 **Baggage** — suitcases and other luggage, and the articles in them, taken on your **journey** or bought during it, that belong to you (not borrowed or hired).

2.2 **Carrier** — any airline, ferry, train or coach operator with whom you hold a ticket for travel on your **journey**.

2.3 **Certificate of Insurance (Certificate)** — the document issued to you showing the policy number, insured persons, cover level, destination area, period of insurance and wording reference.

2.4 **Excess** — the amount you must pay towards each claim, per insured person, per section, per incident. The amount of the **excess** depends on your cover level and is shown in the Table of Benefits.

2.5 **Essential items** — replacement clothing, toiletries, prescribed or over-the-counter medication, and phone or device chargers and travel adaptors, which you reasonably need because your **baggage** is temporarily lost. Items intended to replace what you would have packed, of similar type and reasonable value. Fashion, luxury and outerwear items exceeding the reasonable needs of the **journey** are not **essential items**.

2.6 **Gadget** — mobile phones, smartphones, tablets, laptops, e-readers, smart watches, cameras, headphones and similar portable electronic equipment.

2.7 **Journey** — a trip that begins when you leave **home** and ends when you return **home**, within the dates shown on your Certificate.

2.8 **Home** — your usual place of residence in the United Kingdom.

2.9 **Medical practitioner** — a registered doctor, other than you or a relative of yours, who is legally licensed to practise medicine in the country where the treatment is given.

2.10 **Pre-booked excursion** — a sightseeing tour, activity, event or excursion at your destination that you booked and paid for before the **journey** started, or during the **journey** before the incident giving rise to the claim.

2.11 **Public place** — any place the public has access to, including but not limited to beaches, parks, streets, shops, restaurants, bars, airports, stations, hotel lobbies and public transport.

2.12 **Unattended** — when you are not within arm's reach of your property or **valuables**.

2.13 **Valuables** — jewellery, watches, furs, binoculars, **gadgets**, and any article made of or containing gold, silver or precious metals or stones.

2.14 **We will pay** — the maximum amount we will pay for each insured person, after deduction of any **excess**, as shown in the Table of Benefits for your cover level.

## 4. Section A — Cancellation

A.1 **What is covered.** We will pay up to the amount in the Table of Benefits for your share of unused travel and accommodation costs which you have paid or are contracted to pay and cannot recover, if you necessarily cancel your **journey** because of one of the reasons in A.2.

A.2 **Covered reasons:** (a) death, injury or illness of you, a travelling companion, a close relative or a person you were going to stay with; (b) you are called for jury service or as a witness; (c) your **home** is made uninhabitable by fire, flood or storm within 7 days before departure; (d) you are made redundant, provided you qualify for payment under redundancy legislation.

A.3 **Not covered:** (a) the **excess**; (b) any medical condition you knew about at the time you bought this policy unless declared and accepted by us; (c) you not wanting to travel; (d) costs recoverable from any other source, including the **carrier**, tour operator or a credit card provider.

---

## 5. Section B — Curtailment

B1.1 We will pay up to the amount in the Table of Benefits for your share of unused travel and accommodation costs that you cannot recover if you have to cut short your **journey** and return **home** early because of a reason listed in A.2.

B1.2 **Not covered:** the cost of excursions, tours or activities, whether pre-booked or not.

---

## 6. Section C — Emergency medical and associated expenses

C1 **What is covered.** We will pay up to the amount in the Table of Benefits for the following necessary and reasonable costs incurred outside the United Kingdom as a result of you suffering unforeseen injury or illness during the **journey**:

C1.1 emergency medical, surgical and hospital treatment and ambulance charges;
C1.2 medication prescribed or recommended by a **medical practitioner**, and over-the-counter medication or medical supports (for example bandages or supports) bought to treat the injury or illness on the advice of the **medical practitioner**;
C1.3 emergency dental treatment for the relief of pain only, up to £300.

C2 **Repatriation.** The cost of bringing you home if our medical officer agrees it is medically necessary.

C3 **Local travel.** Reasonable taxi or public transport costs to and from the place of treatment, up to the C3 sub-limit in the Table of Benefits. Receipts must show the date, fare and route or destination.

C4 **Hospital daily benefit.** £25 (Silver) for each complete 24 hours you are an in-patient abroad.

C5 **Excess.** The C1 **excess** applies once per insured person per incident and applies to the total of C1 and C3 costs for that incident.

C6 **Reciprocal health agreements.** If you are travelling within the European Union and you use a UK Global Health Insurance Card (GHIC) or EHIC to reduce your medical costs, we will waive the C1 **excess**. The **excess** is not waived where treatment is received privately without use of a GHIC/EHIC.

C7 **Not covered under Section C:**
C7.1 any medical condition you knew about when you bought the policy unless declared to and accepted by us;
C7.2 non-medical items bought at a pharmacy, including but not limited to sunscreen, cosmetics, toiletries, food, drinks and vitamins, even if bought on the same receipt as covered medication;
C7.3 treatment that can reasonably wait until you return **home**;
C7.4 costs of any person not named on the Certificate;
C7.5 the **excess**.

## 7. Section D — Personal belongings and valuables

D.1 **What is covered.** We will pay up to the amount in the Table of Benefits for the value or repair of any of your own **baggage** or **valuables** that are lost, stolen or damaged during the **journey**, after deduction of an amount for wear, tear and loss of value (see D.5).

D.2 **Limits.** The most we will pay for any one article, pair or set of articles is the single article limit. The most we will pay for all **valuables** in total is the **valuables** limit.

D.3 **Conditions.** You must:
D.3.1 report loss or theft to the local police within 24 hours of discovery and obtain a written police report;
D.3.2 provide proof of ownership and value (an original receipt, bank statement or similar) for any item valued over £100;
D.3.3 report loss or damage by a **carrier** to that **carrier** and obtain a Property Irregularity Report.

D.4 **Not covered:**
D.4.1 the **excess**;
D.4.2 loss or damage due to wear and tear, depreciation, mechanical or electrical breakdown;
D.4.3 **valuables** or **gadgets** left in checked-in **baggage**;
D.4.4 **gadgets**, unless the optional Gadget extension (D.6) is shown on your Certificate, in which case cover is provided under D.6 instead of D.1;
D.4.5 loss or theft of any property or **valuables** left **unattended** in a **public place** or in an unlocked vehicle;
D.4.6 loss or theft not reported to the police within 24 hours of discovery;
D.4.7 sports equipment while in use;
D.4.8 property delayed, detained or confiscated by customs or other officials.

D.5 **Wear, tear and loss of value.** We will settle claims on an indemnity basis. The deduction will be calculated using the depreciation scale in our Claims Handling Guidelines, applied to the original purchase price.

D.6 **Gadget extension (optional — only if shown on your Certificate).** We will pay up to £1,000 (Silver) for the accidental loss, theft or damage of your **gadgets**, subject to an **excess** of £50 per claim. Exclusions D.4.5 and D.4.6 apply to the Gadget extension.

## 8. Section E — Delayed baggage

E.1 **What is covered.** If your **baggage** is delayed on your **outbound** journey for more than 12 hours, we will pay up to the amount in the Table of Benefits for the cost of **essential items** you buy during the delay.

E.2 **The delay period** is measured from the actual time of arrival of your outbound flight or other transport at your destination to the time the **carrier** returns your **baggage** to you, as confirmed in writing by the **carrier** or its handling agent.

E.3 **Conditions.** You must:
E.3.1 obtain a Property Irregularity Report (PIR) from the **carrier** at the time you discover the delay;
E.3.2 obtain written confirmation from the **carrier** of the date and time your **baggage** was returned to you;
E.3.3 keep receipts for all **essential items** you claim for.

E.4 **Not covered:**
E.4.1 items bought after your **baggage** was returned to you;
E.4.2 items that are not **essential items**;
E.4.3 delays on your return journey;
E.4.4 any amount the **carrier** has paid or has agreed to pay you.

E.5 If your **baggage** is later confirmed permanently lost, any amount paid under Section E will be deducted from your claim under Section D.

---

## 9. Section F — Personal money and passport

F.1 We will pay up to the amounts in the Table of Benefits for loss or theft of your personal money and passport during the **journey**. Conditions D.3.1 and exclusion D.4.5 apply.

---

## 10. Section G — Travel delay and missed departure

G.1 **Travel delay.** If your outbound or return departure is delayed by the **carrier** by at least 12 hours, we will pay the benefit in the Table of Benefits for each full 12 hours of delay.
G.2 **Missed departure.** We will pay reasonable additional travel and accommodation costs to reach your destination if you arrive too late to board because public transport fails or the vehicle you are travelling in breaks down or is involved in an accident.

---

## 11. Section H — Personal liability

H.1 We will pay up to the amount in the Table of Benefits for your legal liability for accidental injury to a person or accidental loss of or damage to property during the **journey**.

---

## 12. Section I — Legal expenses

I.1 We will pay legal costs to pursue compensation for your death or personal injury caused by a third party during the **journey**, provided there are reasonable prospects of success.

---

## 13. General exclusions (apply to all sections)

GE.1 Any claim arising from a medical condition not declared and accepted by us.
GE.2 War, terrorism (except as provided under Section C), nuclear risks.
GE.3 Travel against the advice of the UK Foreign, Commonwealth & Development Office.
GE.4 Your alcohol or drug use where it leads to the claim (except prescribed drugs).
GE.5 Your deliberate exposure to needless danger, except in an attempt to save human life.
GE.6 Any loss which is not directly covered by the terms of this policy (consequential loss).
GE.7 Any cost you would have had to pay had the event giving rise to the claim not occurred.

---

## 14. General conditions (apply to all sections)

GC.1 **Making a claim.** You must notify us of any claim within 31 days of returning **home**, provide the documents we reasonably request at your own expense, and give us all the information we reasonably need.

GC.2 **Fraud.** If any claim is in any way fraudulent or exaggerated, or if you or anyone acting for you makes a false statement or uses false documents, we will not pay the claim, may cancel the policy, and may share information with fraud prevention agencies.

GC.3 **Other insurance.** If any loss is also covered by another policy, we will only pay our proportionate share.

GC.4 **Recovery from others.** We may take over and pursue any claim you have against a third party, in your name, at our expense.

GC.5 **Currency.** All claims are paid in pounds sterling (GBP). Where you incurred costs in another currency we will reimburse the sterling amount actually debited to you, or, where that cannot be shown, convert at our published reference rate for the date the cost was incurred.

GC.6 **Payment.** We will pay claims to the policyholder named on the Certificate, to the bank account verified by us. We will not pay to a third party unless you and we have agreed so in writing.

GC.7 **Duty of reasonable care.** You must take all reasonable steps to protect yourself and your property and to minimise any claim.

## 15. Complaints

If you are unhappy with our decision, contact Larkspur Customer Relations at the address on your Certificate. If you remain unhappy, you may refer your complaint to the Financial Ombudsman Service within six months of our final response.
